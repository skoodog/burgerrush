# Asset manifest

Every asset in this build is **generated procedurally at boot** by
`buildProceduralAtlas()`. There are no binary art or audio files in the
repository. Final source art replaces a painter behind the same texture key;
nothing else in the game changes.

## Naming

```
chef.<part>[.<variant>][.<accent>]      chef.torso.red, chef.face.sal.girl.hurt
enemy.<species>.<part>                  enemy.stalker.body
ingredient.<kind>.<raw|pressed>         ingredient.lettuce.pressed
stage.<element>                         stage.platform, stage.ladder
boss.<part>                             boss.bunTop, boss.armour.patty.cracked
shot.<family>                           shot.pickle, shot.spatula
ui.warn.<family>                        ui.warn.mustard
fx.<effect>                             fx.puff, fx.crumb, fx.spark
prop.<item>                             prop.spatula, prop.whisk
```

Accent-bearing keys exist in three variants: `.red`, `.blue`, `.neutral`.

## Enemy parts

Per species (`stalker`, `brat`, `phantom`, `ringletSmall`, `ringletLarge`):
`body`, `arm`, `leg`, `foot`. Plus a shared `enemy.stunStar`.

## Ingredients

`bunTop`, `bunBottom`, `lettuce`, `cheese`, `patty`, `tomato`, `pickle`, `onion`
— each in `raw` and `pressed`, 16×12 per tread segment.

## Boss

`boss.bunTop` 96×44, `boss.bunBottom` 96×36, `boss.face` and `boss.faceEnraged`
96×40, `boss.armour.<lettuce|cheese|patty|tomato>` and their `.cracked` variants
92×16, `boss.port.<family>` 22×14.

## Projectiles and warnings

`shot.pickle` 12×12, `shot.ketchup` 28×12, `shot.mustard` 16×12,
`shot.mayo` 16×16, `shot.spatula` 14×7.
`ui.warn.<pickle|ketchup|mustard|mayo>` 16×16 — shape-coded glyphs.

## Audio

Synthesised at runtime by `AudioDirector`. Cue ids match the prompt headings in
`docs/ART_AND_AUDIO_PROMPTS.md`: substrate footsteps (11 surfaces × 4
variations), tread, layer armed/drop/land, enemy carried, burger complete,
portal, map flip, spatula pickup/throw/hit, klaxon, launch, four condiment
telegraphs, armour break, vulnerable core, enrage, boss defeat, timer tick, final
warning, life lost, round clear, UI move/confirm/back, plus a four-layer adaptive
music bed.

## Provenance

Nothing in this build was generated by an external service. `docs/ASSET_LEDGER.md`
is empty by design and no credits were spent.
